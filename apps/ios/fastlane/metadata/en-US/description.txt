Disbigle is a free, open source social network that connects you to the whole fediverse from a single timeline: follow and talk with people on Mastodon, diaspora*, Friendica, Hubzilla, Pixelfed, Misskey and many more.

NO ADS, NO ALGORITHM
Your timeline shows the posts of the people you follow, newest first. Nobody decides what you see, and nobody sells your attention.

YOUR PRIVACY, YOUR RULES
• Aspects: group people (Family, Friends, Work…) and choose who sees each post, as on diaspora*.
• Public, followers-only or private posts.
• Content warnings and blurred sensitive media.
• No tracking: the app only talks to your own server.

EVERYTHING YOU NEED
• Posts with photos, polls, mentions and hashtags, with buttons for bold, headings and lists, and a preview before publishing.
• Private messages as a chat, with "Seen" and the status of the people you write with, if they share it.
• Photo albums and a Media tab on every profile.
• Post translation on the server, without sending texts to outside services.
• Link previews without tracking.
• Notifications, bookmarks, liked posts, "My activity".
• Hide posts and hashtags, mute or block people, report content to the moderators.
• Light and dark themes and eight colours to choose from.

BRING YOUR ACCOUNT
Coming from Mastodon or diaspora*? Move here from the website: your followers find you on their own.

TRULY OPEN
Disbigle is free software (AGPL-3.0 licence): the code is public on GitHub and anyone can run their own server. The app works with app.disbigle.com and other Disbigle servers.

Sign-ups on app.disbigle.com are approved by a moderator, to keep spam and fake accounts away.
